# AGENTS.md

Floridex is a pocket flower index: photograph a flower, an on-device model names it among 516 species and the app registers it with its Spanish card. Two apps share one model and one data set: `web/` (Vite, TypeScript, transformers.js, offline PWA) and `android/` (Kotlin, Compose, CameraX, ONNX Runtime). `ml/` trains and evaluates the model; `shared/` holds the shipped model and data. User docs are in `README.md`, build and test details in `docs/development.md`, the human workflow in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Rules
- User-visible text is peninsular Spanish with tuteo, never voseo; no em dash, en dash or middle dot in it. Everything else in git is English: comments, script output, docs, commits, issues, PRs.
- Photos and progress never leave the device: no network call with user data, no CDN, no analytics.
- The web and Android apps must behave the same: a change to identification, capture or storage lands in both, with a test in both.
- Both apps prepare photos exactly as training does (`web/src/preparar.ts`, `Clasificador.preprocesar`); do not swap in a platform resize.
- Checks before a PR: `cd web && pnpm build`, `cd android && ./gradlew assembleDebug assembleDebugAndroidTest`, `uvx ruff check ml`. Run the end-to-end checks in `docs/development.md` for anything touching identification, storage or the camera.
- Conventional Commits, trunk-based, squash merge; see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Design
Palette: classic handheld LCD greens (#0f380f, #306230, #8bac0f, #9bbc0f) on a leaf-green shell, A and B in berry red; type Pixelify Sans. Chosen 2026-11-21 by the user: "estilo retro y pixelado", a collectible index, not a herbarium. Photos have two views, chosen with the «Vista» switch (user's rule, 2026-11-21): normal, as they are; pixel, sprites and photos in the four greens. Silhouettes and the interface are always pixelated. Do not copy Pokémon names, art, sounds or the red device look.

## Data
- One copy of the app data and the model, in `shared/`: `floridex.json`
  (built by `floridex.py`), `sprites/`, `miniaturas2/` (reference photos),
  `modelo/`. The web (`web/scripts/copy-assets.mjs`) and Android (Gradle task
  `copiarDatos`) copy them at build time; those copies are gitignored.
- Reference photos are only iNaturalist photos under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA,
  each with its entry in `miniaturas2/creditos.json` (author, licence,
  observation URL). A photo without a credit is never used: `floridex.py`
  drops it and its sprite, and the flower falls back to the generic pixel
  flower. Sprites are derived works of those photos and carry the same
  credit. Each flower's entry shows «Foto: <author>, iNaturalist», linked to
  the observation, next to its photo, sprite or silhouette; the small
  pictures in the list and in the choice menu link nowhere, their entry does.
  The web footer says where the photos come from.
- Photos from the training datasets are never published: the test samples
  (`android/app/src/androidTest/assets/`, `android/emisora/src/main/assets/`)
  are generated by `shared/muestra.py` and gitignored.
- Numbers are frozen in `shared/numeracion.json`: never renumber; new flowers
  take the next free numbers.
- The app icons (`web/public/icono*`, `android/.../drawable/ic_flor.xml`) and
  the generic pixel flower are original pixel art, not derived from any photo.
